# Update 1: ideas (MAYBE, not decided)

Yaani's idea, 9 Oct 2026. **Nothing here is promised or scheduled.** It is written down so it is not lost.
Nobody builds any of it until Yaani says so and it is turned into jobs in `docs/RELEASE_TASKS.md`.

## The idea, in Yaani's words (tidied)

- A **portal on the base** (by the fence, near the gate) that leads to a second place of the player's own: a
  sky base or bunker, with more to do.
- **Parkour** there, so a player has something to do with their hands while the towers farm, and does not
  leave.
- **Mini cannon battles against other players**, 1v1 or 2v2, to make the game competitive. With **combat
  enchantment keys**, **combat enchantments** and perhaps **separate combat mini cannons**.

## Why it is worth having

The game plays itself: once the towers are up, the player watches. That is the moment a child leaves. Both
halves of the idea give them something to do in that moment, and battles give a reason to care how good
their mini cannons are beyond a bigger number.

## Claude's thoughts (suggestions only)

**Parkour: the cheaper half, and the better first step.**
- An obby above the base, reached by the portal: floating platforms in the world's own style, so each of
  the 12 worlds gets its own course for free from the island kit.
- The base keeps farming while the player climbs. A small HUD line shows the level and coins ticking, so
  they never feel they are missing out.
- Checkpoints, and a chest at the top once a day (gems, a key, a crate). A harder course per world.
- A timer and a best time per course, with a leaderboard: competition without any combat code.
- Risk: falling must never cost anything, and the course must be finishable on a phone.

**Battles: the bigger half. Three ways to do it, cheapest first.**
1. **Auto-battle (recommended).** Each player picks 3 mini cannons; they fight by themselves in a small
   arena while both players watch. Stats come from the mini cannon's damage, tier and enchantments. No
   aiming, no lag problems, fair on a phone, and it reuses the models and the loadouts that exist.
2. **Race.** Both players' mini cannons shoot the same row of targets; the first to clear it wins. Simple,
   but it is just "who has more damage".
3. **Real-time PvP** with the player aiming. The most exciting and by far the most work: hit detection,
   lag, cheating and balance. Not for a first update.

**Keeping battles fair.** Raw damage runs from hundreds to billions, so a new player could never win.
Battles should use a mini cannon's rarity, tier and enchantments on a flat scale, not its farming damage.

**Combat enchantments.** Reuse the enchanting table and keys that exist rather than a second system: add a
"combat" family of enchantments (shield, first strike, heal, stun) that only does something in battles. A
separate Combat Key (for gems) rolls only those. Separate combat-only mini cannons would double the models
to make; using the ones players already own is cheaper and makes their collection matter more.

**Rewards.** A battle currency or trophies, spent on things that do not make the farm stronger (titles,
auras, base skins), so battles cannot be farmed for progress. A weekly ranked ladder.

**Things to be careful with.**
- Matchmaking needs enough players online at once. Offer a battle against a saved copy of another
  player's team when nobody is around.
- 2v2 needs friends or matchmaking for four: do 1v1 first.
- Wagering items on a battle would be gambling between children: no stakes, only rewards from the game.
- Losing must not feel bad for small children: no loss of anything, and a consolation prize.

## A possible order, if it goes ahead

1. The portal and one parkour course on Earth, with a daily chest. (Small.)
2. Courses for the other worlds and best times. (Medium, mostly Blender themes.)
3. Auto-battle 1v1 against a saved team, with trophies. (Large.)
4. Live 1v1, combat enchantments and the Combat Key. (Large.)
5. 2v2 and a ranked ladder. (Later.)
